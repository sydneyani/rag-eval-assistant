# Letter to shareholders

## Fourth-quarter results {q4-results}

This shareholder letter is dated February 20, 2025 and discusses the quarter ended February 2, 2025. Fourth-quarter fiscal 2024 revenue was $1.36 billion, compared with $1.05 billion in the fourth quarter of fiscal 2023. Fourth-quarter fiscal 2024 gross margin was 63.0 percent. The company shipped 1,780 HIR-3 racks in the fourth quarter of fiscal 2024.

The quarterly revenue figure is not the annual figure. Annual fiscal 2024 revenue of $4.82 billion is disclosed in the Form 10-K, and this letter does not recompute it. Readers who add the fourth quarter to an assumed run rate will not recover the year, because the first three quarters are not itemized in this letter. This letter gives the fourth quarter only.

Gross margin of 63.0 percent in the quarter is higher than the annual gross margin of 61.4 percent reported in the Form 10-K. The difference is mix inside the year. The letter does not present a reconciliation beyond that statement. Software attach, discussed below, was higher in the fourth quarter than in the prior-year quarter, and management cites that mix as the reason the quarterly margin sits above the annual margin.

The 1,780 racks shipped in the quarter are a subset of the 6,150 HIR-3 racks shipped in the full fiscal year. The average selling price for the quarter is not disclosed separately. The annual average selling price of $428,000 is a Form 10-K figure and is not restated as a quarterly price.

## Fiscal 2025 guidance {guidance}

The company guided fiscal 2025 revenue to a range of $5.55 billion to $5.75 billion. The company guided fiscal 2025 gross margin to a range of 62 percent to 64 percent. Guidance was issued with this shareholder letter on February 20, 2025. Guidance is forward-looking. It is not a guarantee of results, and it is not a revision of the fiscal 2024 results already reported.

The revenue range sits above fiscal 2024 revenue of $4.82 billion. The company did not guide net income, operating income, or capital expenditures. A question about guided net income is not answered by this letter, because no such guide was given. The gross-margin range is above the fiscal 2024 gross margin of 61.4 percent at both ends of the range's lower bound: 62 percent is the bottom of the guide, and 61.4 percent is the year just reported, so the guide does not include a decline.

Management listed three conditions that would put results outside the range: a denial of export licenses on high-memory configurations, a failure to renew the Quarry Ridge power purchase agreement on workable terms, and a reduction in orders from Customer A. Those conditions are the same risks the Form 10-K describes. The letter does not assign probabilities to them.

The letter does not guide the software attach rate. Attach is discussed as a historical quarterly comparison, not as a fiscal 2025 target.

## HIR-4 roadmap {hir4}

The company expects to begin HIR-4 customer sampling in May 2025 and to reach general availability of HIR-4 in October 2025. Sampling means selected customers running production-like jobs on early racks. General availability means the company will accept ordinary purchase orders for HIR-4. Neither date is a Form 10-K disclosure. The Form 10-K records research and development expense and explicitly does not state these months.

HIR-4 is the successor to HIR-3. The letter does not disclose an HIR-4 average selling price, a memory threshold, or a shipment target. Questions about the $428,000 price or the 6,150 unit count are questions about HIR-3 in fiscal 2024, and they are answered in the Form 10-K products section, not here. The only HIR-4 facts in this letter are the May 2025 sampling start and the October 2025 general availability.

A slip in silicon bring-up would move both dates. The company did not describe a slip as of the letter date. The research and development spending that funds this work is the $890 million reported in the fiscal 2024 Form 10-K. This letter does not repeat that expense total.

## Software attach {software-attach}

In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2024, 64 percent of rack shipments included a Helios Fabric Compiler subscription of one year or longer. In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2023, that software attach rate was 51 percent. Attach is measured on racks shipped in the quarter, not on the installed base. A renewal of a subscription on an older rack does not count in the attach rate. Renewals are in software and support revenue and are not in this percentage.

The 64 percent figure is a fourth-quarter rate, not a full-year rate. The company does not disclose a full-year attach rate in this letter or in the Form 10-K. Citing 64 percent as the fiscal 2024 attach rate would overstate what was disclosed. The comparison that is disclosed is quarter against quarter: 64 percent versus 51 percent.

Management connects the higher attach rate to the quarterly gross margin of 63.0 percent. The connection is qualitative. The letter does not state how many margin points came from attach. Fabric Compiler is the product named in the subscription. Northline Scheduler is not sold as a separate subscription after the acquisition. Northline-derived revenue of $188 million is a Form 10-K figure for the full year and is not the same number as the attach rate.
